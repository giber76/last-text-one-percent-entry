Entry for [make.fun's Last Text at One Percent bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) (`u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876`).

`main.ts` writes the last text message a phone sends at one percent battery to `/output/entry.txt`:

> 1%. you watched six episodes on me and NOW you reach for the charger? i'm dying out of spite. goodbye.

The joke is the phone's grudge: it sat through six episodes without a
complaint, and only now — at one percent — does its owner reach for the
charger. So it dies out of spite. The voice is the phone's own, the
detail is specific, and at 102 characters it wastes nothing.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
